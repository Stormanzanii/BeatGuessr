How to play

  1. Press Play to hear a 0.1-second clip.
  2. Type the song title, then press Enter or Guess. You can also select a suggestion.
  3. Wrong guesses and Skip advance to 0.5, 2, 8, then 15 seconds. Skip at 15 seconds reveals the answer.
  4. Press Next song to keep playing. Use the collection panel for genre, year, and playlist options. Check multiple sources to pool their songs.

Provider previews are excerpts, usually from the middle of a song. Add local audio to hear actual intros.

Songs with unknown years only appear with All years. Songs with unknown genres appear under All genres or Unknown. Your settings and played-song history stay in this browser.
